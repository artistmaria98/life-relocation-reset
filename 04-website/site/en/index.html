FAQ

Questions worth clarifying before booking.

Is this a visa consultation?

No. This is a strategic coaching session and personal direction diagnosis. It does not replace a lawyer, tax adviser, immigration specialist, or financial professional. Where relevant, Masha can help you identify trusted specialists and contacts who can support those specific questions.

Do I need to already know the country?

No. Often the session is useful precisely because you need to understand what matters most: country, market, income, environment, freedom, relationships, career, or a new life structure.

Can we discuss work and income?

Yes, at a strategic level: which markets, work formats, skills, networks, and first steps may fit your situation.

What language is the session in?

English and Russian.

Important boundary

Life Relocation Reset is a strategic session based on lived international experience, practical research, and a coaching approach. It is not legal, tax, immigration, or financial advice, and it does not guarantee visas, jobs, income, residency, or specific introductions.
